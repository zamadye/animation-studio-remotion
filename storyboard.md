# Storyboard — Studio Satir

Eight fictional Indonesian scripts · 48 scenes · 30 fps · 90 s target per episode.

## Global direction
- Custom template family: CARTOON-CUTOUT-EDITORIAL. IDs and structural variants are enumerated in template-spec.json.
- First scripted action is the 0–2 s hook; no separate unsubstantiated claim is inserted.
- Focal priority changes per template: acting, source dialogue, or named prop.
- Scene cuts occur on source shot boundaries; no music/SFX/VO has been synthesized.
- Crop priority is face/hands/evidence; three ratios use independently authored layout parameters, not a center crop.
- All motion is deterministic from local frame and typed shot data.
