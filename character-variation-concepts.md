# Character variation concepts — preliminary

Rendered concept sheet: `character-variation-concepts.png` (1080×1920).

Eight reusable cast designs from existing episode data are shown with distinct palette, hair, costume and silhouette choices: Nina and HR (Episode 02), Ibu Sari and Bendahara (Episode 04), Mira and Humas (Episode 05), Dini (Episode 06), and Rika (Episode 08). These are exploratory character variations using the current shared rig, not final model sheets or approved designs. Full hand, wardrobe, and acting refinement remains subject to the main proof acceptance review.
