# Creative brief — Studio Satir

## Goal
Build an editable Remotion animation studio around eight supplied Indonesian satirical scripts. The current deliverable is a frame-driven concept animatic framework, not a final episode.

## Audience and format
Mobile short-form audience. 30 fps; 90 seconds per script; independently composed 9:16, 1:1, and 16:9 output layouts. Script dialogue/duration are estimates and must be re-timed against recorded VO.

## Direction
Original flat cutout-vector style with restrained editorial typography. Three structural variants: staged dialogue, evidence/prop insert, and phone/message POV. Movement is motivated by acting cues, prop focus, or scene entry; no stock assets, decorative camera shake, or unmotivated transitions.

## Technical
Remotion 4.0.545. Frame state is the only animation clock. Design tokens and source data are local. Reduced motion is available in the studio UI.

## Unknowns / assumptions
- Unknown: final approved model sheets, brand tokens, VO/SFX/music and exact platform safe zones.
- Assumption: authored DOCX timecodes are scene lengths; speech will be re-timed after recording.
- Fiction note: all scripts state they are fictional; keep the disclosure in deliverables.

## Missing final deliverables
- Render MP4, contact sheet and audiovisual QA are NOT PRODUCED because the Remotion headless browser cannot download in this environment.
- Character art is concept art, not approved production model sheets.
